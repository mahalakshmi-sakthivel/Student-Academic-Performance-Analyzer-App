Timed Explanation Script (2 Minutes • Word-for-Word)

0:00 - 0:25
1. Introduction & Problem Statement

"Welcome to the Student Academic Performance Analyzer. In educational institutions, tracking student marks, attendance, and subject arrears across various semesters often leads to scattered data and manual calculation errors. This application solves that problem by providing a fast, offline-first dashboard that analyzes student academic records and delivers actionable insights in real time."

0:25 - 0:50
2. Assessment Model & Calculation Engine

"At the core of the system is a transparent calculation engine. Each subject is evaluated using four normalized components: Internal marks, Assignments, Unit Tests, and Final Semester Exams. The subject score is calculated, and the overall student performance combines the subject average at 85% with attendance at 15%. In addition, a 10-point CGPA scale is automatically computed."

0:50 - 1:15
3. Subject Arrears & Personalized Recommendations

"A key innovation is independent subject arrear detection. Even if a student achieves a good overall score, any subject below 50% is highlighted with a clear red arrear alert. The system also runs a rule-based advisor that generates personalized recommendations, advising students on attendance improvement, exam preparation, and their weakest subjects."

1:15 - 1:40
4. Class Analytics & Multi-Level Filtering

"For educators and mentors, the Class Analytics dashboard provides immediate class-level visibility. It includes interactive KPI cards and supports filtering across 14 engineering departments, sections A, B, and C, academic batches, and semesters. With the built-in demo generator, over 2,900 realistic student records can be analyzed instantly."

1:40 - 2:00
5. PDF Reporting & Desktop Deployment

"Finally, the reporting module produces structured, print-ready A4 PDF reports with a live preview before saving. Packaged as a native Windows desktop application with Electron, it requires no internet connection or external server, ensuring complete privacy, reliability, and ease of use. Thank you!"
